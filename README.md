# Bloodroot 🌿

A text-based CLI game merging dynasty management of Crusader Kings 
with colony simulation, built to strengthen core Java fundamentals.

## Story

After his father falls in battle against the Almorgh Church, a young elf 
chief flees religious persecution with his people, sailing to the newly 
discovered continent of **Novamor**. Now he must manage the settlement, 
navigate faith and politics, and prepare for the day the Church comes 
looking for them again.

## What it is

- A turn-based simulation where each turn represents a season
- Manage villagers, resources, and buildings to keep your settlement alive
- Navigate dynasty mechanics —  marriages, advisors, character traits
- Handle event-driven storytelling: refugees, missionaries, plagues, 
  and the looming threat of an Almorgh Church expedition
- Built to strengthen core Java concepts: OOP, collections, streams, 
  custom exceptions, and file-based persistence
